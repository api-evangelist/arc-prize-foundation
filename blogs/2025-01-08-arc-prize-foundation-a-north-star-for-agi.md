ARC Prize Foundation - a North Star for AGI *Foreword from ARC Prize Co-founders Mike Knoop & François Chollet:* ARC Prize launched in 2024 as an experiment to promote the relatively obscure, but important, ARC-AGI benchmark. Now, after our OpenAI o3 testing, nearly everyone in tech has heard of it. But there is much more work to do to reach AGI.
