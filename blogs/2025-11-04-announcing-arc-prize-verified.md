Announcing ARC Prize Verified Today we're announcing **ARC Prize Verified**, a program to increase the rigor of evaluating frontier systems on the ARC-AGI benchmark. In addition to certified score verification, this program adds a third-party academic panel to audit and open source our testing process. We are also excited to welcome 5 new AI labs as sponsors of ARC-AGI-3, our third-generation Interactive Reasoning Benchmark.
