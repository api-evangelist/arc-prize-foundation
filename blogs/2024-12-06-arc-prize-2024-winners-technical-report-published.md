ARC Prize 2024 Winners & Technical Report Six months ago, we launched this crazy $1M experiment called ARC Prize to test an idea: are new ideas needed for AGI? While the Grand Prize went unclaimed this year, today is a very exciting day as we announce this year's 2024 winners and publish the ARC Prize 2024 Technical Report. In total, 1,430 teams submitted 17,789 entries for ARC Prize 2024.
