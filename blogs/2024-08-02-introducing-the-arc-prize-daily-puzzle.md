Introducing the ARC Prize Daily Puzzle **Wordle meets AI IQ test.** ARC Prize Daily Puzzle Puzzles are fun. Viral online puzzle games like the New York Times' time-tested Wordle and the newly launched, AI-generated chess variant Echo Chess deliver a daily hit of dopamine to all those who take a moment out of their day to solve a brain teaser. Since the launch of ARC Prize, we've put ARC-AGI tasks front and center.
